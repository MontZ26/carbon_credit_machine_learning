Fusion Architecture
End-to-end data flow from raw inputs to variance-weighted fused surface
Step 1–4: Sensor Preprocessing
Raw CSV → cleaned daily means
Filter MAGNITUD == 8 (NO₂ only)
Validate V-flag check per hourly reading
Aggregate Daily mean (≥ 18 valid hours)
Merge Station metadata + UTM coords
CRS Re-project to EPSG:25830 (UTM 30N)
Steps 6–9: Ordinary Kriging
Spatial interpolation across 100 m grid
Model Exponential variogram
Grid 100 m resolution, −3.825→−3.53°W
Output NO₂ mean surface + kriging variance
Export GeoTIFF (EPSG:25830)
Coverage Full Madrid bounding box
Steps 10–18: TROPOMI Extraction
Sentinel-5P via Google Earth Engine
Product OFFL/L3_NO2 — column density
Extraction Station-level + full spatial grid
Units mol/m² → µg/m³ conversion
Align KD-Tree nearest neighbor (Step 19)
Date 2025-01-14 overpass
Step 20: Bias Correction
Satellite calibrated against 24 ground stations
Linear Regression R² 0.067
Low spatial variability at TROPOMI resolution (3.5 × 5.5 km) makes regression unreliable with only 24 points. Switched to bias correction.
Mean Bias (sensor − satellite) +18.4 µg/m³
Correction applied Additive offset
Step 21: Uncertainty Weighting
Kriging variance drives sensor/satellite blend weights
The normalized kriging variance field controls how much each source contributes at each pixel:
w_sensor = 1 − σ²_norm
High near stations where kriging uncertainty is low → favours ground measurements
w_satellite = σ²_norm
High far from stations where kriging uncertainty is high → favours satellite data
